# C-Tetouan location and photograph provenance

Editorial verification: 10–11 October 2026. The executable registry is `dist/location-data.js`. Captions, coordinate notes and coverage explanations are available in French, Arabic, English and Spanish.

## Coordinate policy and sources

The map contains 22 source-backed location references. Coordinates were taken from named published objects or records, not inferred from photographs, fabricated, or assigned to guessed district centres. A marker is a geographic reference; it does not establish neighbourhood boundaries, a public entrance, opening hours, accessibility, or a walking route. The four-language preview notes explain when a street, square, mosque or stadium is used as the reference for exploring a district.

- OpenStreetMap records: © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Named object IDs, decimal coordinates and lookup metadata were cached during bounded editorial Nominatim research with an identifying User-Agent and spaced requests. The visitor-facing website never queries Nominatim.
- [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/about.html): [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Six retained references were compared with the exact rows in the downloaded primary [Morocco gazetteer](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/MA.zip), rather than with approximate third-party map centres. GeoNames coordinates use WGS84.
- [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Copyright): structured data under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The Bab El Oqla and ethnographic museum values were compared with their fresh P625 API statements on 11 October 2026.
- Tamuda: [Ideophagous’s published camera GPS at the northern Roman gate](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tamuda_northern_gate_of_the_Roman_military_camp.jpg), 35.559835, −5.410938. This replaces a coarse gazetteer point that did not coincide with the photographed excavated site. The point is explicitly a camera reference at the northern gate, not the ticket-office entrance.

The Saniat Rmel record uses the named stadium in OpenStreetMap way 150999458. Two cached lookups return the same stadium object and coordinates. Its note explicitly distinguishes the stadium landmark from the district and its boundaries. Stadium photographs retain the club’s dates and captions.

The Martil reference is the published town-centre label, not a beach entrance. The mountain reference for Jbel Dersa is not a trailhead. Feddan Park is distinguished from Place Hassan II in front of the Royal Palace; the palace record identifies the public square and does not promise access inside the palace.

Rbat Aala, Rbat Asfal, Essania and Sidi Bahrouri remain available as place pages, but no marker is invented for them. Published points with similar names were rejected when their subject was different: for example, GeoNames Sidi El Bahrouri is a cemetery, and Houmat Essania is a separate locality away from the historic medina.

## Photographic selection

The registry adds 32 distinct location image sources: 24 optimized local Creative Commons/CC0 photographs and 8 credited remote images whose publishers retain their rights. Six correctly identified photographs from the original collection are reused in specific-site galleries. All seven non-district attraction pages have at least three genuinely distinct images; additional verified district/landmark galleries also contain three or more. Photographs are not duplicated through crops to inflate a gallery.

The old generic craft-shop photo is not labelled as the ethnographic museum. A generic medina passage is not labelled as Dar El Oddi. Broad views are not used as exact photos of Taboula, Jamaa Mezouak, Mhannech, Boujarrah, Touibla or Samsa. When an exact subject and attribution could not be verified, coverage is explicitly unavailable and the UI uses a labelled placeholder.

The El Ayoun image is a source-captioned broad view of El Ayoun and the Ensanche from Dersa, taken in May 2008; its archive date is stated in all four languages. Its inconsistent Commons camera GPS is deliberately **not** used for a map coordinate. The Dersa gallery distinguishes a city view from Dersa, the kasbah wall, and the former barracks visible from the city.

Dar El Oddi’s courtyard, exhibition entrance and gallery images come directly from the venue’s [official website](https://dareloddi.com/?lang=fr). They are remote credited images, with no claim that an open reuse licence exists. Dar Sanaa’s façade is the Commons school photograph released under CC0. Its two workshop images are Scott Ponemone’s own [documented 2018 visit](https://www.scottponemone.com/tetouan-art-i-see/): they are clearly labelled photographic collages of pattern studies and embroidery, not current single-frame views. They remain remote, credited and rights reserved. The image host linked by the ministry’s Dar Sanaa page was unreachable and was not added as a broken gallery source.

The three official MAT stadium photographs are reused from `footballDetails.stadium.photos`. See [FOOTBALL.md](FOOTBALL.md) for their dated match and renovation sources. A stadium photograph is identified as the stadium, not as a photograph of the entire Saniat Rmel district.

Local JPEGs are proportionally resized to at most 2560 pixels on the longest side and compressed, with no upscaling. The author, original file page, licence link, actual served dimensions, original dimensions and change notice are retained for every new local asset. CC BY-SA derivatives retain their original share-alike licence. A small original is never described as Full HD. Remote images retain their native sizes, rights and publisher-hosted URLs; they are not bundled as new freely licensed assets. External hosting may change or fail, so the UI handles image errors with a labelled fallback.

## Verification performed

All 22 coordinate values were checked against retained primary-source data: 13 OSM object lookup records, 6 Morocco GeoNames rows, 2 Wikidata P625 statements, and Tamuda’s published GPS fields. All photo IDs and gallery references resolve. All 32 newly registered image URLs/sources are distinct. The 24 local JPEGs have different SHA-256 hashes and actual Pillow dimensions matching the registry; no local asset exceeds its original dimensions. The new official/photographer images and the local image contact sheets were visually inspected to verify their depicted subjects. Captions and geographic notes were checked for all four website languages.

Browser interactions, map lifecycle, tile failure handling, desktop/mobile layouts and publication are verified separately by the root integration workflow. Research caches and contact sheets are ignored development files; runtime visitors do not trigger coordinate research, scraping or paid API requests.
