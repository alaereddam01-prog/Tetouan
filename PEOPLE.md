# People of Tétouan: editorial and image provenance

Reviewed on 11 October 2026. `dist/people-data.js` contains 22 unique profiles in French, Arabic, English and Spanish. Categories overlap where appropriate: Fayçal Azizi has one profile appearing in music and screen, and Marta Baizán appears in both categories. Each profile has its own visual asset, original short biography, explicit relationship to Tétouan and linked references.

## Editorial approach

Birthplace, residence and artistic training are distinct. Mariano Bertuchi was born in Granada and worked in Tétouan. Mohamed Melehi was born in Asilah and studied in Tétouan. Their profiles do not call them Tétouan-born. The historical biographies distinguish documented events from later legend; al-Wazzani’s Arabic Quixote translation is described as incomplete. Al-Haik’s eighteenth-century work cannot have an original voice recording.

Research sources include UNESCO, El Legado Andalusí, university papers, BnF, Instituto Cervantes, the Recording Academy, the Goya Awards, the performers’ sites, galleries and Mathaf Encyclopedia. Individual URLs are in each profile rather than a shared generic biography citation.

## Historical likenesses

- Ali al-Mandri: a genuine photograph of his Tétouan mausoleum. This is explicitly labelled a site photograph, not his likeness.
- Sayyida al-Hurra: a modern artistic representation by Oxygene Tetouan, explicitly labelled as a representation rather than an authenticated or contemporary portrait.
- Abdessalam Bennuna: a portrait painted by Mariano Bertuchi, labelled as a painting.
- Mohammed al-Haik: a page from an actual **1908 manuscript copy** of his Kunnash, catalogued by INLIBRIS. It is neither his autograph nor his likeness. The manuscript date is visible in all four captions.
- Mohammed Daoud: the archival portrait published by Ma3lama. The enhanced colour image cached from TetouanClub was deliberately not used because its original photographic provenance was unclear.

No AI faces, synthetic portraits or generic repeated city images are used for people.

The data states that copyright remains with the source and that no open reuse licence was stated. Source pages are linked and the photographer is marked unidentified where necessary. ## Validation

All 22 visuals were inspected against the named source. Local image hashes are distinct, actual dimensions were decoded from JPEG/PNG headers, all 22 profiles have complete language fields and portrait metadata, and `node --check dist/people-data.js` passes. Browser-level card, media, accessibility and responsive tests belong to the integrated site’s release checks.
